Privacy Policy — Yoté

Last updated: 9 September 2026

In short

Yoté requires no account and collects no personal data of its own. It shows advertising served by Google AdMob between some games: that is the only part of the app that reaches the network, and the only part that transmits anything.

Advertising

The app embeds the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To select and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • the device advertising identifier
  • IP address, device model, Android version and language
  • ad events: impression, click

This information is collected and used by Google, not by us: we only see aggregate figures (impressions, revenue). We never receive it individually and never combine it with anything.

Google's processing is described at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings (Google → Ads). The app keeps working; ads simply become less relevant.

Data kept on your device

The app stores the following in its private folder:

  • your settings: volumes, interface size, move hints, vibration, chosen

    • mode and level;

  • the game in progress, so it can be resumed
  • your statistics and challenges: games played, won, lost, drawn, streaks,

    • pieces captured, challenges completed;

  • a game counter used to space ads out

These files never leave the device. No server receives them. You can erase the statistics from the app's "Progression" screen, and remove everything by uninstalling the app.

What the app does not do

No account, no sign-up, no in-app purchases. No analytics of our own. No access to contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. The game itself is played offline.

Backup

Android automatic backup is disabled: game data is not copied to your Google account.

Children

The app suits all ages, but it shows advertising and is not designed for children under Google Play's Families policy.

Android permissions

INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID, required by the advertising SDK. Nothing else.
